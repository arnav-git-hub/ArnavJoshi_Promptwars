## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-craft digital utility: razor-sharp, human-centered minimalism engineered for deep focus, swift navigation, and quiet confidence. Drawing inspiration from modern developer-grade and productivity tools, it discards superfluous decoration, skeuomorphic noise, and sci-fi tropes in favor of pristine typographic discipline, tactile micro-interactions, and calibrated dark-mode hierarchy.

The visual style unites **Minimalism** with subtle **Low-Contrast Tonal Layering**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian canvas layered with zinc surfaces, whisper-thin 1px hairline dividers, and controlled luminescent accents.
- **Tone & Mood:** Purposeful, surgical, calm, and effortlessly modern. Content and workflows take precedence, supported by immaculate information density.
- **Target Audience:** Discerning professionals, software engineers, product designers, and power users who expect instantaneous feedback, clear visual affordance, and zero interface fatigue.

## Colors

The palette establishes a meticulous dark tonal architecture constructed from neutral zinc and deep carbon tones:

- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - `surface-base` (`#09090b`): The deep floor canvas absorbing ambient distraction.
  - `surface-card` (`#18181b`): First-layer container for cards, panels, sidebars, and grouped content.
  - `surface-elevated` (`#27272a`): Interactive element hover fills, popovers, dropdown menus, and command palettes.
  - `border-hairline` (`#27272a`): Precision 1px separator lines and structural boundaries.

- **Monochrome Typographic Hierarchy:**
  - `text-primary` (`#fafafa`): High-clarity primary text, active headers, and decisive labels.
  - `text-muted` (`#a1a1aa`): Standard body text, descriptive summaries, and primary metadata.
  - `text-dim` (`#71717a`): Micro-labels, disabled states, key hints, and breadcrumb dividers.

- **Semantic Indicator Accents:**
  - Status indicators avoid harsh primary saturation; instead, they pair deep, desaturated background chips with luminous, readable tint text.
  - **High Severity / Critical:** `risk-high` (`#f43f5e`) indicator dot with `risk-high-subtle` (`#3b111a`) container fill and `risk-high-text` (`#fda4af`) foreground.
  - **Medium Severity / Warning:** `risk-medium` (`#f59e0b`) dot with `risk-medium-subtle` (`#382305`) container fill and `risk-medium-text` (`#fef3c7`) foreground.
  - **Low Severity / Healthy:** `risk-low` (`#10b981`) dot with `risk-low-subtle` (`#062e22`) container fill and `risk-low-text` (`#a7f3d0`) foreground.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual architecture. Titles and labels utilize **Geist** for crisp structural cadence, geometric clarity, and deliberate letterform reduction. **Inter** serves body text contexts, ensuring optimal readability across extended documentation and data-dense tables. **JetBrains Mono** is reserved strictly for hash keys, keyboard shortcuts, timestamps, and metric IDs.

- **Optical Kerning:** Large headlines employ negative letter spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.035em`) to eliminate optical whitespace gaps and produce an authoritative, cohesive graphic presence.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Line heights match fixed 4px/8px baselines to maintain strict vertical alignment across multi-column dashboards.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Headline levels use pure `#fafafa`; running body text stays anchored to `#a1a1aa` to preserve eye comfort in dark contexts; meta notes and secondary descriptors rest at `#71717a`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, mathematically consistent 4px/8px fluid grid architecture that adjusts to content density:

- **Structural Grid:** 12-column dynamic desktop layout with 24px-32px gutters, collapsing into a 6-column grid on tablet and single-column stacked hierarchy on mobile devices.
- **Information Density:** Compact padding configurations are standard within data tables and operational feeds (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), while higher-level workspace containers, overview cards, and editorial headers utilize broad, open breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-2xl`).
- **Responsive Viewport Transitions:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Margins pinned to `margin` (16px), navigation collapses into a bottom action sheet or top drawer, gutters standard at 16px.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Margins expand to 32px, sidebars collapse to icon-only rails.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width capped at 1440px with balanced centering, pinned sidebars, and fluid multi-pane workspace panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation in this design system avoids heavy shadows, blur overload, or heavy gradients. Depth is established through **calibrated tonal step-ups** and **1px hairline borders**:

1. **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Canvas backdrop (`#09090b`) with no borders or shadows.
2. **Surface Layer (Elevation 1):** Cards, canvas segments, and list containers render at `#18181b` enclosed by a crisp, continuous `1px solid #27272a` hairline border.
3. **Floating Layer (Elevation 2):** Flyouts, dropdown menus, quick search modals, and command bars leverage `#18181b` with an interior border of `1px solid #3f3f46` coupled with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
4. **Interactive States:** Interactive card hover states do not physically elevate; rather, they subtly highlight their outline from `#27272a` to `#3f3f46` accompanied by an imperceptible background tint increase (`#1c1c20`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework favors modern, refined curvings that soften the visual field without feeling bubbly or childlike:

- **Micro Components (8px / `0.5rem`):** Badges, inline code pills, chips, nested list triggers, and standard form input controls.
- **Macro Components (12px / `0.75rem` to 16px / `1rem`):** Standalone cards, modal dialogs, flyout sheets, and notification banners (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Command triggers, status badge pills, and floating toolbar caps may implement fully rounded caps (`9999px`) when paired with micro-typography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-contrast crisp white background (`#fafafa`), solid black label text (`#09090b`), font weight 500, height 36px, `border-radius: 8px`. Hover: `#e4e4e7` with smooth 150ms ease transition. Active: subtle scale down (`0.98`).
- **Secondary (Subtle Zinc):** Background `#18181b`, border `1px solid #27272a`, text `#fafafa`. Hover: background `#27272a`, border `#3f3f46`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#a1a1aa`. Hover: background `#18181b`, text `#fafafa`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Background `#09090b`, border `1px solid #27272a`, height 36px, text `#fafafa`, placeholder `#71717a`. Focus state: border `#fafafa`, subtle outer white ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #fafafa`, zero ambient halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle, `#18181b` fill with `1px solid #3f3f46`. Checked: `#fafafa` fill with `#09090b` check icon mark.

### Badges & Risk Meters
- **Structure:** 20px height, compact padding (`4px 8px`), inline flex, `border-radius: 9999px` or `6px`.
- **High Risk:** Background `#3b111a`, text `#fda4af`, 1px border `#f43f5e33`. Accompanied by a 6px pulsing dot in `#f43f5e`.
- **Medium Risk:** Background `#382305`, text `#fef3c7`, 1px border `#f59e0b33`. Dot in `#f59e0b`.
- **Low Risk:** Background `#062e22`, text `#a7f3d0`, 1px border `#10b98133`. Dot in `#10b981`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#18181b` background, `1px solid #27272a` perimeter, rounded 12px corners. Headers feature a bottom border `1px solid #27272a` dividing the title block from the body content. Hoverable cards illuminate their hairline border to `#3f3f46`.

### Lists & Tables
- Borderless table rows separated only by `1px solid #1f1f23` horizontal divider rules. Hovering a table row triggers a discreet `#18181b` surface fill across all child cells. Column headers strictly formatted with `label-sm` in `#71717a` with uppercase letter tracking (`0.05em`).

### Command Palette (Spotlight)
- Centered modal layout at 640px width, floating backdrop filter `blur(12px) saturate(180%)`, surface fill `#18181be6`, border `1px solid #3f3f46`, featuring inline keyboard shortcut indicators styled with `JetBrains Mono` inside `#27272a` keycaps.
